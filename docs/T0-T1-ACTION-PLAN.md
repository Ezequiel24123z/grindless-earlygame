permits bounded technical closure and independent GREEN work while the owner is absent.

and then use the following GREEN classes where they expose a genuine gap:

- Semantic recipe/provider/slot/byproduct and prohibited-prototype contracts.
- Reproducible test classpaths and real PowerShell/Bash tooling parity.
- Deterministic encountered-asset production, provenance and resource validation.
- Accurate source/evidence documentation and mapping of already implemented routes.

Do not duplicate already-covered data variants or reopen settled systems. A01/A02 and
the A03 transition/cache boundaries must be reconciled against current code/evidence,
not assumed unfixed. A concrete remaining bug may be repaired without balance changes.

intermediates and broad T1 content remained held. The owner has not accepted/frozen T0. The official Superflat bootstrap,
generator/shop economy, human feel and longitudinal acceptance are not silently selected
