# T0 Bootstrap: connected workshop

**Status: IMPLEMENTATION IN PROGRESS under ADR-0129.** This is the active owner-directed
T0 rework, not a declaration of survival acceptance. The preserved foundation below describes
the starting implementation; its old no-expansion restriction no longer applies.

## Active workshop contract

Keep the physical five-F1-Matrix exit, F0 power and existing saved identities. Add an original
ceramic route before newly crafted Data Cores: gravel/brick milling, clay/sand-or-grog mixing
with water, then fuel-backed batch firing. Two ceramic insulators and one reusable grog come
from three unfired pieces and one carbon fuel. Grog returns to the Mixer with fresh clay/water;
insulators serve cores, plumbing and primitive handling. Early quest rewards must not skip it.

The Firing Chamber is a controller plus seven brick blocks in a 2x2x2 volume. Its controller
occupies the front-left bottom corner. A second unit adds an open three-block Settling Trough:
controller, channel, screen, all facing the same way and with clear space above. It supplies
clay and gravel from dirt/water and returns part of the water; this is game abstraction, not
a claim of chemical mass balance. All process interfaces remain on the controller.

The existing unpowered pump, clay pipe, tank, belt and manipulator are reused rather than
forked into duplicate transport engines. Their T0 material routes must not consume a Matrix.
The two-iron Dynamo/Extractor budget remains narrow; it excludes optional vanilla equipment.
The target is now **35-60 minutes from the material entry**, explicitly unmeasured.

Required machine behavior: correct F0 buffer/rate, input/output slots, fluid arrival waking an
idle consumer, no recipe progress transferred to a different recipe, retained work while
starved or structurally invalid, atomic output reservation, no partial ingredient consumption,
orientation-safe formation and unloaded-cell refusal. New menu kinds append to existing
ordinals. Existing indexed inventory and Matrix codecs remain authoritative.

The graph JSON is an audit projection of live recipe catalogues, never a second quantity source.
Extend checks to cover actual new runtime content rather than retaining the earlier
"new_runtime_* must be empty" preparation gate. Check useful sinks and non-amplifying loops.
Keep all new recipes discoverable through the native Atlas, guide, quests and optional JEI.

protocol, including the corrected indexed inventories and rated F2 output.

T0 has **no new multiblock**: a framework or decorative structure would violate its current
process role and the T3 Arc Reactor schedule. No fluid, gas, pressure, heat maintenance,
hazard or waste item is added merely to populate a specification field.

## Materials, byproducts and logistics

Use existing tagged iron/copper, stone/planks/sticks, glass and redstone, plus the two cores
and Relay Matrix. No new material forms are accepted in this preparation. The present core/
Matrix routes have no byproducts; do not manufacture waste solely to claim a recycling chain.

The real opening logistics choices are where to extract, where to buffer the output, how to
allocate one F0 source, and when to retrieve/relocate a machine. A chest beside the Extractor
provides useful output automation without a new controller. Later belts, industrial renewal,
fluids and recycling remain their named tiers. Quest rewards are one-off evidence/rewards,
not repeatable production inputs or proof of renewable supply.

## Rework objectives, deliberately not final recipes

The richer slice should increase connected decisions and clarity, not stack counts:

1. Make the entry bill of materials and physical exit discoverable without reading source.
2. Teach power allocation by operating extraction and calibration, including a controlled
   starvation/recovery step, rather than adding another hand-cranked machine with the same role.
3. Teach buffering and blocked-output recovery with existing inventories before requiring belts.
4. Explain what the first five Matrices fund and why the Press is necessary before the Assembler.
5. Require any proposed new intermediate to serve at least two justified downstream uses, and any
   new byproduct to have a reachable sink and an automation plan before adding its recipe.

Two product decisions remain open: whether "significantly richer" extends the current roughly
twenty-minute target, and whether a genuine new primitive material/chemical process justifies
superseding the existing entry contract. Neither is inferred from a general request for more
content. The [external research](../research/tiers/t0-research.md) supplies principles, not copied
recipes or permission to move T1 industry or T3 multiblocks into T0.

not an active build-out queue while the gate is closed.
