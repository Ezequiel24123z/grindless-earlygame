# T0 Bootstrap: connected workshop

**Status: implementation in progress; T0 is not accepted.** The [README](../../README.md) is
the high-level design authority. This page records the current detailed T0 contract, not a new
recipe catalogue or a survival-acceptance claim. The active gate and evidence limits are in the
[go gate](../reviews/go-gate.md), [handoff](../../.agent/handoff.md) and
[validation record](../testing/latest-validation.md).

## Current objective

T0/F0 establishes a connected primitive workshop and the first mod-owned geological/biological
source base. Its physical exit is a calibrated Data Core and one recoverable five-F1-Relay-Matrix
batch, crafted by hand. The F0 Assembly Bench automates the limited Bootstrap catalogue and four
vanilla basics; it never makes a Relay Matrix. This is implemented behavior, not proof that the
whole tier has been played or accepted.

ADR-0159 sets the entry contract: the first quest is free and provides a generous
one-time stone, wood, food, water and basic-material kit. The quest book must be
available from initial entry without paper, commands or a selected village.
`QuestCatalogue` owns the allocation and the existing server claim ledger owns
the per-player/world claim; the UI must show the complete reward.

This is finite construction bootstrap, not a permanent farm or an automatic grant
of finished machines/cores. Normal gathering and a furnace remain optional
compatibility routes. A Hand Crank Dynamo and Crude Extractor using two iron in total
are an optional quick start, not required entry costs or the renewable source route.
The Extractor's chunk vein remains finite. The new entry implementation does not
by itself prove client interaction, pacing or a no-command campaign.

## Implemented route and authorities

The Java catalogues are the quantity authority. This summary names route roles only; it does not
duplicate recipe bills.

| Route | Live authority | T0 boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geological aggregate and clay | `SourceLogic`, `RenewableProcesses` | Geological Collector requires a valid terrestrial rock bed and environmental conditions; it does not renew finite chunk veins. |
| Biomass and primitive energy | `SourceLogic`, `RenewableProcesses` | Sunlit Biocultivator grows biomass from aggregate and water; the Biomass Dynamo converts biomass to F0 power. |
| Aggregate, metal fractions, redstone, biomass products and glass/reduction routes | `RenewableProcesses` | These are the implemented mod-owned T0 source and conversion routes. Do not infer S2 recursive closure or all-condition autonomy from their existence. |
| Ceramics, grog recovery, fired bricks and settling | `BootstrapProcesses` | Quern, Clay Mixer, Firing Chamber and Settling Trough form the connected workshop. |
| Bootstrap crafting and Data Core | `BootstrapRecipes` | The Data Core uses ceramic insulator and redstone; the former stone-and-redstone recipe is superseded. |
| Physical calibration | `ResearchLogic`, `ResearchTerminalBlockEntity` | The Research Terminal converts a physical Data Core to a physical Calibrated Data Core; it does not grant a world permission. |
| Primitive automation | `BootstrapAssembly` | The Assembly Bench projects the Bootstrap catalogue and four vanilla basics, but no Relay Matrix route. |
| Five-matrix exit | `T1Recipes.shaped(): relay_matrix` | This remains the manual physical T0 exit. The exact input bill belongs to the catalogue. |

The native Atlas uses `AtlasLogic.allEntries`: generated processes, calibration, Bootstrap
shaped/shapeless crafts and the T1 shaped catalogue. It is not an exhaustive vanilla crafting
viewer. Actual client/JEI discovery remains an acceptance check. Quest objectives check item
possession, not provenance or the
construction of every intermediate; `calibrate.requires=[ceramics]` and
`relay.requires=[calibrate]` remain intentional. The physical Core recipe and Research Terminal
define production. Player guidance must explain that route without treating a quest as a global
permission or a manufacturing certificate.

`quern` requires `multitool`, leaving `crank` and `extractor` as an optional branch with their
existing IDs/rewards. The correction is implemented in `cc3f111`; its conditional reward and
single-claim behavior are checked without adding a quest barrier for calibration intermediates.

## Workshop geometry and I/O

- **Firing Chamber:** one controller plus seven brick blocks in a fixed 2x2x2 volume. The
  controller is at the front-left bottom corner. The controller owns the machine interfaces.
- **Settling Trough:** controller, channel and screen in one facing line, with open space above
  the passive channel and screen. The controller owns item/fluid interfaces.
- **Other workshop machines:** Quern, Clay Mixer, Geological Collector, Biocultivator, Biomass
  Dynamo and Assembly Bench are single-block machines.

Use the existing supported adjacent inventory, fluid and power interactions. General
replaceable ports/hatches and ME integration are not implemented T0 capabilities. Do not infer
them from a controller or a structure drawing.

The existing shared machine contracts preserve work while starved or structurally invalid,
reserve outputs before consuming a batch, and retry after relevant input/output changes.
Structure orientation, exact formation, persistence and full player-facing behavior must not be
claimed beyond their recorded source-bound proofs.

## Evidence and acceptance boundary

The S1 audit core, C3 native restoration/cycle behavior and renewable-source cycle each have
source-bound evidence. The native source proof is bounded to a daylight window; it is not proof
of downstream power autonomy, nighttime restart, complete water independence, full S2 canonical
data/closure, a no-command survival playthrough, actual client interaction or saved-world
compatibility. Those remain pending. Do not turn prototype inventory or a bounded proof into
accepted T0/T1/T2 progress.

The current 35–60 minute target is unmeasured and starts from material entry, not a bare-world
spawn. It is a pacing target, not observed playtime. The survival-acceptance checklist and current
gate remain the acceptance sources.
