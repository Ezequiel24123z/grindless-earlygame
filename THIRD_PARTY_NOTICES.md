no FTB implementation is copied. XMod Compat is a functional JEI dependency:
it installs the recipe helper that the quest core alone does not provide.
Compilation and native binding against the selected jars remain separate evidence
from source/API inspection, and client recipe clicks still require client validation.

## Required entry for every future import

Add an entry before committing the imported material. Include:

- upstream project name and canonical URL;
- exact tag, release or commit used;
- upstream licence and a copy or durable reference to its required notice text;
- the files or assets imported and how they were changed;
- attribution, source-disclosure, licence or distribution obligations; and
- the reviewer who confirmed the intended distribution complies with those terms.

Do not list design inspiration that copies no protected expression. See
[ADR-0109](docs/DECISIONS.md#adr-0109--upstream-material-may-be-reused-under-its-own-licence)
for the policy.
