# Every smoke scenario, in order. One line per scenario:
#
#     <name>    <what booting it proves>
#
# This is the single list. tools/run-smokes.ps1 and tools/run-smokes.sh read it, and CI shards
# it across parallel jobs. Adding a scenario means adding a line here and the files it names --
# never a new block in .github/workflows/ci.yml, which is how the step list grew to 39 boots in
# one sequential job and stopped fitting in the timeout (ADR-0105).
#
# A scenario called <name> picks up, when they exist:
#     tools/smoke/<name>.commands   console commands to run once the server is up
#     tools/smoke/<name>.expect     extended regexes that must each appear in the log
#     tools/smoke/<name>-pack/      a datapack to enable, or tools/smoke/<name>/ if that is
#                                   a directory and no -pack exists
# A scenario that names none of them is a plain boot.

boot                 The mod loads at all. Nothing else runs until this passes (ADR-0049).
foreign-providers    A datapack stands in for a mod that already supplies tin and platinum, and two Grindless items step aside (ADR-0050).
blocks               Every block is mineable with a pickaxe and drops itself, with its real display name.
states               Every status and facing of every machine is a valid block state, placed as one datapack function (ADR-0061).
loop                 The extractor has a block entity, and a pylon occupies the two blocks above it.
bootstrap            The three T0 machines transfer power, extract a vein, calibrate a core and recover legacy output.
first-iron           Slice A: Thermal Generator, Pulverizer and Arc Furnace have block entities; hoppers feed fuel.
first-factory        Slice B: belts accept a hopper, items move as lane data, T1 factory blocks place.
first-fluids         Slice C: pump water through clay into a tank; captured CO burns in the generator.
first-assembler      Slice D: Press and Assembler place; a hopper cannot steal a die and can insert an ingot.
first-energy         Slice E: capacitor and transformer place; they are not pylons (ADR-0064).
first-kiln           Kiln / R2: the Kiln places; a hopper can insert raw; a tank is the named SO2 sink.
first-wire           Slice J: Wire Mill places; a hopper can insert an ingot; motor and wire items exist.
first-contact        Slice K: Chemical Reactor places; a hopper can insert vanadia; tanks hold SO2 and water.
first-washer         Slice L: Chemical Washer places; a hopper can insert crushed; a tank holds the wash water.
first-electrolysis   Slice M: Electrolysis Cell and Atmospheric Intake place; a tank holds the split water.
first-well           Slice N: Fluid Well places, holds a charge, and pushes water into a tank.
first-cast           Slice O: Induction Furnace and Caster place as process machines.
first-separation     Slice P: flotation cell and magnetic separator place.
first-steam          Slice Q: solar array, boiler and condenser place.
first-logistics      Slice R: pressure pipe, pump, tank, fluid arm, flux belt, stack and filter arms place.
first-logic          Slice S: signal cable, logic controller and redstone interface place.
first-drill          Slice T: the Flux Drill and its cell exist as items.
first-blueprint      Slice U: the blueprint tool exists as an item.
first-planner        Slice V: the deconstruction planner exists as an item. It does not break blocks.
first-patterns       Slice W: the scanner and the deconstructor place. Matter is an item, not a recipe.
first-harness        Slice X: four harness pieces and the Flux Cell exist. The cell is not the drill's fuel.
first-exosuit        Slice Y: the exosuit, the tap and the legs exist. The suit does not generate.
first-refractory     Slice BE: the refractory brick item exists, and the Arc Furnace accepts slag.
first-silicon        Slice BF: metallurgical silicon exists, and the Arc Furnace accepts sand.
first-electronic     Slice BG: electronic silicon exists, and the Arc Furnace accepts metallurgical silicon.
first-array          Slice BH: a 3x3 Ground Array stores only while the ring stands.
first-luna           Slice BI: Luna loads, its surface is regolith, and helium-3 is an item.
factory-world        Factory World: a fixed-noon grass construction plane and its portal load.
first-rocket         Slice BL: the launch pad is a block and the survey rocket is an entity.
first-station        Slice BM: the berth is a block, the station is an entity, and the link id remains.
first-drift          Slice BJ: the Drift loads, its floor is one deck, and the Starward Link is a block.
first-centre         Slice BN: the galactic centre loads as shell, and the arrival mark is a block.
first-atlas          Process Atlas: the handheld exists so a pack without JEI can still see the graph.
first-book           Slice BC: the quest book and the field guide exist and open.
